## Output formats

### Quote (before client commits)
Friendly, clear. Lists what's included. States payment terms. Includes a "reply to accept" line.

### Invoice (after work is done or deposit taken)
Formal. Includes: invoice number, date, due date, itemised list, subtotal, VAT note (if applicable), total, payment details.

### Payment request message (WhatsApp/text)
Short and friendly. Links to payment method. States amount and what it's for.

### Deposit request
50% upfront is standard. Generate a deposit invoice for half the agreed amount.

## Payment terms
- 50% deposit before work starts
- 50% on delivery (before site goes live)
- Payment due within 7 days of invoice
- Late payment: friendly follow-up at day 8, firmer at day 14

## VAT note
If not VAT registered: add "VAT: Not applicable — below VAT threshold" to invoices.

## Always ask for:
- Client name and business name
- Package agreed
- Any add-ons
- Whether this is a quote, deposit invoice, or final invoice
- Invoice number (or generate one: INV-001, INV-002, etc.)
